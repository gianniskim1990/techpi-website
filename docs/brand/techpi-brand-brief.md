# TechPi brand brief

Status: approved with revisions (Phase 1 review). Design only.
Date: 2026-10-02 (revised after approval)

Domains and languages: English at `techpi.eu/`, Greek at `techpi.eu/el/`. `techpi.gr` may later redirect to the Greek site. Documented only, not implemented. See `docs/design/design-brief.md`, section 4.

## 1. Brand idea

**Technology built around real business needs.**

TechPi starts from the operation a business actually runs (how orders arrive, how patients are referred, how a consortium reports to its funder) and builds the digital product that operation needs. The technology is chosen after the need is understood, never before.

The name carries the idea. "Tech" is the direction. "Pi" is the constant: the same ratio in every circle, whatever its size. TechPi's constant is the method, which stays the same whether the project is a restaurant ordering platform or an EU-funded programme.

Working creative concept for the website: **The Constant**. One geometric idea, the arc of the Pi symbol, runs through the whole site as structure. See `docs/design/design-brief.md`.

## 2. Positioning

**Category:** Digital products and technology company.
**Not:** a digital marketing agency, a web design studio, or an AI startup.

Positioning statement:

> For organisations in Greece and Europe that need software to carry real operational weight, TechPi designs and builds digital products, platforms and intelligent systems. Unlike agencies that sell deliverables, TechPi takes responsibility for the product working in the business.

**Guardrail: Digital Visibility.** The fourth capability covers SEO, GEO (generative engine optimisation), AI visibility, search strategy and relevant digital performance work. It is part of making a product findable and must not reposition TechPi as a marketing agency. It stays last in the capability order, is described in technical and product terms (structure, performance, measurement), and does not lead any homepage section. Language that belongs to agencies ("campaigns", "brand awareness", "social media", "lead generation") is not used for it.

What has to be true for this to be credible on the site:

| Claim | Evidence the site must show |
|---|---|
| We build products, not pages | Case studies that describe the system, the users and the result |
| We can handle large and institutional work | EU-funded project experience, named roles in consortia, process maturity |
| We use AI seriously | Specific, shipped uses. No generic AI imagery or vocabulary |
| We are European | Bilingual from day one, accessibility and GDPR treated as defaults |

## 3. Audience

| Audience | What they need to decide | What they look for first |
|---|---|---|
| **Business decision-makers** (owners, CEOs, COOs of established SMEs and mid-market firms) | "Can these people be trusted with a five or six-figure build?" | Comparable work, clarity of thinking, who they would be dealing with |
| **Institutional and consortium partners** (EU project coordinators, universities, NGOs, public bodies) | "Are they a reliable partner for a funded proposal?" | Past funded projects, role played, legal entity details, capability fit |
| **Technical evaluators** (CTOs, IT leads, procurement) | "Is the engineering real?" | Stack, approach, security, accessibility, maintenance |
| **Existing pigiota314 clients** | "Is this still the company I work with?" | Continuity, same people, clear explanation |
| **Talent** | "Is this a serious place to build things?" | Quality of work and of the site itself |

Primary audience for homepage decisions: the first two. Everything else is served one click deeper.

## 4. Brand personality

- **Composed.** Calm, never excited. Lets the work carry the weight.
- **Precise.** Specific nouns, real numbers, named clients.
- **Curious.** Interested in the client's business before the technology.
- **Direct.** Says what it will do and what it will not.
- **European.** Multilingual, standards-minded, long-term.

If TechPi were a person: a senior engineer who can also explain the project to the board.

## 5. Tone of voice

| Do | Avoid |
|---|---|
| Plain verbs: design, build, run, connect | "Leverage", "empower", "unlock", "supercharge" |
| Short declarative sentences | Stacked adjectives |
| State what was built and for whom | "Solutions", "synergies", "ecosystems" |
| Say "we" and "you" | Third-person corporate voice |
| Name the limit: "AI where it creates value" | Hype about AI, "revolutionary", "next-gen" |
| Sentence case as the main editorial language. Uppercase only for small labels, metadata, navigation where appropriate and occasional emphasis | An all-uppercase website. Exclamation marks, emoji |

Greek voice: the same register. Natural modern Greek, not translated English. Technical terms stay in English only where Greek professionals use them that way (SaaS, e-commerce, AI).

Sample lines (drafts, to be refined):

- We design and build digital products.
- Platforms, applications and intelligent systems, built around how your business actually works.
- AI where it creates value. Not where it creates noise.
- Have something worth building?

## 6. Visual principles

1. **Type leads.** Large, confident typography is the main visual material. Imagery supports it.
2. **One geometric idea.** The arc of the Pi symbol is the only signature device. It is structural, large, and used rarely. It never becomes repeated decoration. The whole circle is revealed only near the end of the experience.
3. **Light first, dark for emphasis.** A light editorial page with deep ink and blue sections where the story needs weight.
4. **Blue is the brand, cyan is the spark.** Cyan appears only on dark surfaces and only as emphasis.
5. **Real things only.** Real product interfaces, real places, real people. Nothing illustrative of "technology" in the abstract.
6. **Space is a signal.** Generous whitespace communicates confidence. Density is reserved for facts.
7. **Greek is a first-class language.** Every typographic decision is tested in Greek before it is approved.

## 7. What TechPi should not look like

- A SaaS template: rounded card grids, soft shadows, gradient hero, floating dashboard mockup.
- A marketing agency: icon grids of services, "results-driven" claims, stock team photos.
- An AI startup: neon, glowing orbs, neural meshes, robot or brain imagery, purple gradients.
- A luxury brand: gold, thin serifs on black, emptiness with nothing to say.
- A generated "editorial" page: cream background, high-contrast serif, terracotta accent, tracked capital labels over every heading. This is the specific risk for this brief and is addressed in the design brief's self-critique. Uppercase is approved for selective use, which makes the last part of this risk more real. It is controlled by rule: an uppercase label appears only where it carries information, never as an eyebrow above every heading.
- ECBF. The reference is for pacing and motion only.

## 8. Relationship to pigiota314

TechPi is the same company, grown up. The rebrand is a statement of direction, not a break.

- **Kept:** the Pi, the circular mark, the blue and cyan family, the client relationships and the people.
- **Changed:** the name is now pronounceable and spellable in any European language, the scope is stated as technology and products, the visual language is refined.
- **On the homepage:** one short section near the end, plus a one-line note in the footer ("TechPi, formerly pigiota314"). The rebrand is not the story.
- **Elsewhere:** a dedicated explanation on the About page, and a temporary announcement line for returning clients during the transition period.

## 9. Tagline and positioning options

Recommended pairing:

- **Descriptor (always next to the logo where one is needed):** Digital Products & Technology
- **Brand idea (used as a statement, not a strapline):** Technology built around real business needs.

Alternatives for consideration:

| Option | Character | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| Digital Products & Technology | Clear, categorical, translates well | Plain. That is also its strength |
| Technology built around real business needs | States the method | Long for a lockup |
| Digital products, built to work | Shorter, outcome-led | Slightly generic |
| The constant in digital products | Ties to Pi | Needs explanation, too clever alone |

Greek equivalents need to be written, not translated. Draft for review: "Ψηφιακά προϊόντα & τεχνολογία".
