Gate B lock summary

Final visual decisions

DirectionThe Constant. Arc → circle → TechPi. Three appearances on the page: the hero arc, the edge into Selected work, the Contact resolution.
TypographyCommissioner. Display 450. Body 400. Navigation, labels and buttons 500. A prototype-stage decision until production font files are finalised.
PaletteInk #080E1E, Paper #F0EEE9, TechPi Blue #0841C8. Cyan #1FDDFC as an interaction and focus accent only, with no use at rest.
Surface sequencePaper, Paper, Ink, Paper, Paper, Paper, TechPi Blue, Ink footer. Intelligence is on exact Paper.
HeaderTypographic TECHPI. No symbol. The production header is expected to use a proper flat vector symbol once the identity master exists. No replacement icon was invented.
ContactThe flat symbol at 14%, tone on tone, behind "Have something worth building?". Provisional flat rendering from raster mask.
Selected workOn Ink. One project per viewport. No pills, metrics or dashboard decoration.

Final hero behaviour

  • The statement does not move and is never replaced. "We design and build digital products." is the only headline.
  • On desktop, as the page starts to scroll, the supporting statement "Technology built around real business needs." rises once from behind a line mask, beneath the statement, at about a quarter of its size and in the secondary text colour. What we do, then why we do it this way.
  • The hero holds for half a screen of scroll, then releases. The arc drifts slightly with the scroll. Scrolling is native throughout.
  • On mobile, with reduced motion, or without scripting, there is no hold and the supporting statement is simply present.

Removed in the final restraint pass

  • The raster-mask symbol in the header.
  • The two-headline exchange in the hero. The second statement is now a supporting line.
  • The hairline rule and the "Selected work" link at the foot of the desktop hero. The navigation already offers Work.
  • The Sheet surface. Intelligence is on Paper, set apart by one hairline and by space.
  • The "Selected work" label repeated on the second and third projects. The counter remains.
  • The "All capabilities" link. The navigation already offers Capabilities.
  • The descriptor repeated in the footer.
  • The display-weight preview switch in the prototype. The weight is locked.

Added: one hairline above Intelligence and one above Evolution, which replace the colour change as the divider between three Paper sections. Nothing else was added.

Mobile findings (390 px, Greek sample)

  • No horizontal overflow.
  • The Greek hero is readable: four lines at 42 px, weight 450.
  • The supporting statement fits naturally under it in two lines at 19 px, followed by the primary button and one link. All within one 844 px screen.
  • The Contact symbol does not clip. It fits the width with 16 px margins, and the question, button and details do not overlap.
  • The header is balanced without the symbol: name on the left, language switch and Menu on the right. The arc passes between them.
  • Intelligence works on Paper. The hairline and spacing separate it from Capabilities.
  • Checked in one browser engine at one width. Safari, real devices and other widths belong to production QA.

Issues that would block production

No design blocker. The following are inputs production will need. They do not block approval of the design.

  • Identity master. A flat vector symbol, for the header and to replace the provisional Contact rendering.
  • Font files. Self-hosted Commissioner with Greek, which also settles the arrow glyph and lets the tight space before accented Greek capitals be checked properly.
  • Content. Project imagery (demo or redacted data for cAIrelink), confirmed project facts, confirmed AI capabilities, contact and legal details, and reviewed Greek copy.

Arc → circle → TechPi

The circle is the beginning of the TechPi symbol. Its outer sweep is the architectural starting point of the identity. The device has become more specific, not more frequent: it appears at the same three moments as before, but each one is now a stage of the same form.

Provisional flat rendering from raster mask

The logo was not redesigned or traced. The existing transparent PNG's alpha channel is used as a CSS mask over a flat colour, so the exact existing silhouette appears in one colour, without the glossy blue and cyan rendering. It is not the final SVG identity.

Source. The glossy raster asset. Not used in the interface.
Ink on Paper. Not used on the page. Removed from the header.
Paper on Ink. Not used on the page. Shown for completeness.
White on TechPi Blue. Used: the Contact resolution, at 14%.
  • Used in one place only: the Contact resolution, where it is large enough to carry the arc → circle → TechPi idea.
  • Not used in the header. At small size the raster silhouette loses detail and should not become part of the visual language. The header shows a typographic TECHPI. The final production header is expected to use a proper flat vector symbol once the identity master exists.
  • A flat silhouette loses the overlaps that the gloss describes. It still reads as the TechPi symbol, but it is a silhouette, not a designed flat mark.
  • The raster's edge fringing carries through as slightly soft edges at large size. This is a limit of the source.
